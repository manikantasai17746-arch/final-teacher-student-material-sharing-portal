EduVault Privacy Policy

Teacher–Student Academic Material Sharing Portal

Last updated: September 23, 2026

1. Introduction

EduVault is an academic material sharing platform designed to help teachers and students securely share educational materials, assignments, submissions and related academic information.

2. Information We Collect

EduVault may collect information required for account creation and use of the platform, including name, email address, student or employee identification number, academic information, uploaded files and account activity.

3. Google Drive Access

Teachers may choose to connect their Google Drive account to EduVault. When a teacher authorizes Google Drive access, EduVault uses the permissions granted through Google's OAuth authorization process to store and manage academic materials and student submissions in the teacher's connected Google Drive.

EduVault does not ask users for their Google password. Google authentication is handled through Google's OAuth authorization system.

4. How We Use Information

Information collected by EduVault is used to provide authentication, academic material sharing, assignment submission, notifications, file management and other platform functionality.

5. Uploaded Files

Files uploaded through EduVault may be stored in the connected storage configured for the relevant teacher. Teachers can manage materials and submissions through the platform according to their permissions.

6. Information Sharing

EduVault does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the platform, such as database, email, hosting and Google services.

7. Security

EduVault uses authentication, access controls and server-side security mechanisms intended to protect user accounts and academic information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

Account information and academic records may be retained while required to provide the service or for legitimate administrative purposes. Teachers and administrators may manage applicable academic files through the platform.

9. Google Authorization

Users can revoke EduVault's Google account access through their Google Account security settings. Revoking authorization prevents EduVault from using the previously granted Google access.

10. Children's Privacy

EduVault is intended for educational users and should be used under the supervision and policies of the relevant educational institution.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the EduVault service or its data practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page.
