How the Application Works

From first scan to a full material history — four steps, no WhatsApp required.

1

Register once

Teachers sign up with their Employee ID; students sign up with their Roll Number — or just scan their ID card.

2

Scan or log in

Next visit, tap "Scan ID Card." The barcode fills in your ID automatically — no password to remember.

3

Teachers upload

Teachers drop in PDFs, PPTs and notes tagged by subject and unit, straight from their dashboard.

4

Students browse anytime

Students look up any teacher and see everything ever shared — even material posted before they joined.

Why not WhatsApp?

A group chat only shows history from the moment you join it. Join late, change sections, or transfer in as a lateral entry student, and everything shared before you arrived is gone for good.

How EduVault fixes it

Material is stored against the teacher's profile in a database, not inside a disappearing chat thread — so a lookup by Employee ID always returns the full history, no matter when your account was created.
